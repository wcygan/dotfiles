Incident report

A postmortem artifact for communicating what happened, why, who was affected, and what will change.

  • Primary intent: incident report
  • Interaction: sticky navigation
  • Output: timeline and actions

Use when

The user has an outage, SEV, degraded service, security event, or operational failure to summarize.

Input signals

  • "Incident", "postmortem", "SEV", "outage", "root cause", "timeline".
  • Alert timestamps, logs, customer impact, mitigations, owners.
  • Need for follow-up actions and accountability.

Borrow

  • Fact strip for severity, status, duration, detection, owner.
  • TL;DR, minute-by-minute timeline, root cause, impact, action items.
  • Sticky table of contents for long reports.

Avoid

  • Routine weekly reporting.
  • Speculation not labeled as such.
  • Action items without owners or verification.

Pair with

Use `11-status-report.html` for the broader weekly update or `13-flowchart-diagram.html` to explain the failed pipeline/system path.

Validation

  • Timestamps are consistent.
  • Impact numbers and mitigation claims have provenance.
  • Action items have owners and concrete outcomes.
