Staggered Entrance

Four cards enter as a coordinated cascade: fade, slide, scale, and a small overshoot.

Fade in Opacity starts at zero The element appears without popping abruptly into the reading flow.
Slide in Vertical translate creates direction Motion starts below the final position and settles upward.
Scale in Scale adds physical arrival The card starts at 0.96 scale, briefly overshoots, then lands at 1.
Stagger Delay creates orchestration Each card waits 70ms after the previous card, forming a readable sequence.

Motion spec

Purpose
Orient the reader to a new group of related items.
Pattern
Staggered entrance with fade, slide, scale, and pop-in overshoot.
Timing
420ms per item, 70ms stagger, fill mode both.
Easing
Ease-out cubic-bezier for responsive arrival.
