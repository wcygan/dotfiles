Scroll Driven Reveal

Scroll to drive a route drawing, marker movement, parallax depth, and chapter reveal.

Step 1 - Reveal

Content fades and slides into place when it enters the viewport. The purpose is orientation, not decoration.

Step 2 - Scroll linkage

The line dash offset and marker position are tied directly to scroll progress, so the animation is inspectable frame by frame.

Step 3 - Parallax depth

The background grid and route move at different speeds. Depth is subtle so it does not compete with the route.

Constraints
  • One scroll progress value drives all state.
  • Transform and SVG stroke offset animate instead of layout properties.
  • Reduced motion shows the final route without parallax.
